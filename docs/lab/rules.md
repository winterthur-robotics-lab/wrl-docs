or uses its equipment is bound by these rules.

## Safety requirements

**Before your first visit:**

- Complete the general safety briefing. Ask a board member or lab steward to
  run it with you if it was not done on arrival.
- Read the operating instructions posted on each piece of equipment before
  using it.

**At all times in the lab:**

- Wear closed shoes. No sandals or bare feet.
- Tie back long hair and secure loose clothing before operating any rotating
  equipment.
- Do not work alone on equipment that presents a risk of entrapment, crushing
  or electrocution. A second person must be present and aware.
- Do not modify, bypass or disable any safety guard, interlock or emergency-stop
  device.
- Report damage, faults or near-misses to a board member immediately. Do not
  leave damaged equipment in service.

**Robot cells specifically:**

- Maintain a safe distance from robot work envelopes at all times.
- Never enter a robot work envelope while power is on, even if the robot
  appears stationary.
- Confirm that all bystanders are clear before starting any automated sequence.
- If a robot behaves unexpectedly, press the emergency stop immediately — do
  not attempt to intervene physically.

## House rules

- **Access.** The lab is open to members during public evenings (Tuesday
  18:00 – 21:00) and to members who have obtained key access. Non-members
  may visit as guests of a member who is present throughout the visit.

- **Supervision.** Members with key access may bring guests but remain
  responsible for the guest's compliance with these rules.

- **Tidiness.** Return every tool and piece of equipment to its designated
  place before leaving. Leave workspaces clean.

- **Food and drink.** No food or drink near equipment. Closed containers are
  permitted at the desk area.

- **Alcohol and impairment.** No use of equipment under the influence of
  alcohol or any substance that impairs judgment or coordination.

- **Photography.** Photograph your own work freely. Do not photograph other
  members' projects without their permission.

- **Respect.** Treat every person in the lab with respect. The board may
  revoke access to anyone who creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment,
  without prior notice and without refund.

---

## Revoking access

The board reserves the right to revoke access — including key access and
membership — at any time and without prior notice, for breach of these rules
or for any conduct that threatens the safety, reputation or functioning of
the lab. No refund of membership fees or prepaid amounts is owed in that case.
